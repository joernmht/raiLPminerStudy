## 2 Reference model formulation

The proposed optimization model is formulated as a mixed-integer linear programming problem aligning with prior works (Pellegrini et al., 2014, 2015), with the objective of minimizing train delays within the control area. We extended these existing models in our previous work (Lindenmaier et al., 2022) to consider a safety-relevant traffic management regulation, the overlaps. Although the model extension contributes to a safer solution in practice, it can increase the computational cost of the optimization. This runtime overhead can also be reduced by the proposed model decomposition. The rtRTMP to be solved is defined by the trains assigned in space and time to the control area. The multi-layer infrastructure model is constructed from a detailed microscopic graph representation of the rail network, considering the infrastructure’s topological information, as in Lindenmaier et al. (2021). The train schedule is provided based on the fundamental components of the railway infrastructure, the track circuits, where the presence of a train is automatically detected. The track circuits are built from the corresponding edges of the graph model. The group of track circuits delimited by signals form block sections whose consecutive order compose different routes between the boundaries of the control area. The list of optimization parameters is given in Table 1, following the nomenclature of state-of-the-art models in Pellegrini et al. (2014, 2015). The optimization variables of state space, $X$, are listed in Table 2, separated according to the continuous and binary variables.

According to Pellegrini et al. (2014), the model constraints can be distinguished between time concerning, delay, rolling stock configuration related, and capacity constraints considering the railway traffic regulations. Since the focus of this paper is the runtime efficiency, the trains in connection and the constraints due to the change of rolling stock configuration with less impact on the complexity are neglected in our model. In the following, the constraints of the reference model, considering the complete rerouting, reordering, and rescheduling problem, are explained. First, the temporal coherence of train schedules is satisfied by the following time constraints: 

$$ {e}_{r , tc}^{t} \le M{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , r \in {R}^{t} , tc \in T{C}_{r} \tag{1} $$

$$ {e}_{r , tc}^{t} \ge {e}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}^{t} + d{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}^{t}\;{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , r \in {R}^{t} , tc \in T{C}_{r} \tag{2} $$

$$ {e}_{r , tc}^{t} = ini{t}^{t}\;{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , r \in {R}^{t} , tc \in T{C}_{r} : {p}_{r , tc} = t{c}_{0} \tag{3} $$

$$ {e}_{t{c}_{\infty}}^{t} = { \sum }_{ \forall r \in {R}^{t}}{e}_{r , tc}^{t} + d{s}_{r , tc}^{t}\;{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}_{r} : {s}_{r , tc} = t{c}_{\infty} \tag{4} $$

 The ${e}_{r , tc}^{t}$ entry times to track circuits, $tc$, along the $r$ routes not used by train $t$ (${x}_{r}^{t} = 0$) are set to zero by (1). Then, the entry time to a $tc$ track circuit is bounded in (2) according to the ${e}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}^{t}$ entry time of the ${p}_{r , tc}$ track circuit preceding $tc$ along route $r$ and the planned $d{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}^{t}$ time spent on it. Moreover, the exact time when the train $t$ exits the control area (${s}_{r , tc} = t{c}_{\infty}$) is computed according to (4), and the entry time to the control area (${p}_{r , tc} = t{c}_{0}$) is set to the $ini{t}^{t}$ scheduled entry time by the constraint (3).

The delay constraints in (5) and (6) assign the ${d}_{r , tc}^{t}$ local delay to train $t$ at signals ($b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {s}_{r , tc}}$) according to the difference of exact, ${e}_{r , {s}_{r , tc}}^{t}$, and planned entry time to the subsequent track circuit, ${s}_{r , tc}$. Meanwhile, the ${D}^{t}$ total train delay as the basis of the objective function is bounded by the difference between the actual and scheduled exit time. 

$$ {d}_{r , tc}^{t} = {e}_{r , {s}_{r , tc}}^{t} - {e}_{r , tc}^{t} - d{s}_{r , tc}^{t}\;{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , r \in {R}^{t} , tc \in T{C}_{r} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {s}_{r , tc}} \tag{5} $$

$$ {D}^{t} \ge {e}_{t{c}_{\infty}}^{t} - exi{t}^{t}\; \forall t \in T \tag{6} $$

In the following, the capacity constraints that consider the railway traffic and safety regulations are explained. First, the constraint (7) impose that every train utilizes exactly one route. We consider a route lock-sectional release interlocking system with a two-aspect fixed block signaling system, in which a train $t$ reserves all $tc$ track circuit of $b{s}_{r}$ block section with $for$ time in advance before it enters the first track circuit, i.e., $re{f}_{r , tc}$, of $b{s}_{r}$ along route $r$. Moreover, the reservation of a track circuit $tc$ by train $t$ is released only if $tc$ is left by the complete vehicle assembly according to $c{l}_{r , tc}^{t}$, and the $rel$ release time expires. The constraints (8),constr: end res consider these regulations. Finally, the constraints (10)–(12) prevent track circuits from being reserved by multiple trains at a given time. 

$$ { \sum }_{ \forall r \in {R}^{t}}{x}_{r}^{t} = 1\; \forall t \in T \tag{7} $$

$$ s{R}_{tc}^{t} = { \sum }_{\substack{ \forall r \in {R}^{t} : \\ tc \in T{C}_{r}}}{e}_{r , re{f}_{r , tc}}^{t} - for{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \tag{8} $$

$$ e{R}_{tc}^{t} = { \sum }_{\substack{ \forall r \in {R}^{t} : \\ tc \in T{C}_{r}}}{e}_{r , {s}_{r , tc}}^{t} + ( c{l}_{r , tc}^{t} + rel ) {x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \tag{9} $$

$$ {y}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} + {y}_{t , tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} = 1\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} \tag{10} $$

$$ s{R}_{tc}^{t} \ge e{R}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} - M\;{y}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t}\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} \tag{11} $$

$$ s{R}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} \ge e{R}_{tc}^{t} - M ( 1 - {y}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} ) \; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} \tag{12} $$

In railway traffic control, a safety distance, referred to as overlap, is provided beyond stop signals for the trains forced to stop, considering that they might be unable to stop before a red signal due to the driver’s reaction time or increased braking distance. In our previous work (Lindenmaier et al., 2022), we extended the existing model described above with the following overlap constraints to consider this railway signaling concept concerning the overlaps: 

$$ s{O}_{tc}^{t} = { \sum }_{\substack{ \forall r \in {R}^{t} : \\ tc \in T{C}_{r}}}{e}_{r , re{f}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}}^{t} - for{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{13} $$

$$ {z}_{tc}^{t} \ge \frac{1}{M} ( s{R}_{tc}^{t} - { \sum }_{\substack{ \forall r \in {R}^{t} : \\ tc \in T{C}_{r}}}{e}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}^{t} + over{x}_{r}^{t} ) \; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{14} $$

$$ e{O}_{tc}^{t} \ge { \sum }_{\substack{ \forall r \in {R}^{t} : \\ tc \in T{C}_{r}}}{e}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}^{t} + over{x}_{r}^{t} - M ( 1 - {z}_{tc}^{t} ) \; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{15} $$

$$ e{O}_{tc}^{t} \le { \sum }_{\substack{ \forall r \in {R}^{t} : \\ tc \in T{C}_{r}}}{e}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}}^{t} + over{x}_{r}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{16} $$

$$ e{O}_{tc}^{t} \ge s{R}_{tc}^{t} - M\;{z}_{tc}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{17} $$

$$ e{O}_{tc}^{t} \le s{R}_{tc}^{t}\; \forall t \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{18} $$

$$ {c}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} = {c}_{t , tc}^{{t}^{ ' }}\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} \tag{19} $$

$$ e{O}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} - M ( 2 - {c}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} - {y}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} ) \le s{R}_{tc}^{t}\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} : \exists r \in {R}^{{t}^{ ' }} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{20} $$

$$ e{O}_{tc}^{t} - M ( 1 - {c}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} + {y}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} ) \le s{R}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }}\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{21} $$

$$ e{R}_{tc}^{t} - M ( {c}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} + 1 - {y}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} ) \le s{O}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }}\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} : \exists r \in {R}^{{t}^{ ' }} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{22} $$

$$ e{R}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} - M ( {c}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} + {y}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} ) \le s{O}_{tc}^{t}\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{23} $$

$$ y{O}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t} + y{O}_{t , tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} = 1\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} \tag{24} $$

$$ s{O}_{tc}^{t} \ge e{O}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} - M\;y{O}_{{t}^{ ' } , tc}^{t}\; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} : \exists r \in {R}^{t} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{25} $$

$$ s{O}_{tc}^{{t}^{ ' }} \ge e{O}_{tc}^{t} - M ( 1 - y{O}_{{t}^{ ' }0 , tc}^{t} ) \; \forall \{ t , {t}^{ ' } \} \in T , tc \in T{C}^{t} \cap T{C}^{{t}^{ ' }} : \exists r \in {R}^{{t}^{ ' }} : b{s}_{r , tc} \neq b{s}_{r , {p}_{r , tc}} \tag{26} $$

 According to the constraint (13), besides the used block section, the first track circuit following the signal that terminates the section is also reserved for the train. The constraints (15),constr: end overlap12 impose that the track circuit is reserved due to overlap until the overlap time $over$ expires within what the train is assumed to be able to stop safely. However, if the train does not stop (${z}_{tc}^{t} = 0$), that is determined by the constraint (14), the reservation due to overlap finished at time $e{O}_{tc}^{t}$ is bounded by $s{R}_{tc}^{t}$ according to constraints (17),constr: end overlap22. The constraints (19)–(26) prevent the conflicting reservation of a track circuit, similar to the capacity constraints of the original model in (10)–(12). Further details of this model extended with the overlap constraints are provided in Lindenmaier et al. (2022).
